## Result

Lists, menus, the pop-up button, tabs, a split and the scroll area with edge fades came over as add-ons in `src/ui/containers/`, each a behaviour and its look; lists and menus read keys as the platform's commands, not key codes. The `ui` budget is split: the UI core at 2734 of 3500, widgets and containers at 1311 of a new 2000. Scrolling the 100k-row list costs 0.19 ms a frame on the Mac at scale 1 (0.54 at 2) and 1.2 ms on mele (4.4 at 2), view to raster in the harness; in a window on Wayland it holds 60 fps while scrolling and wakes 0 times when still. Reviews brought the core four small hooks (`Press::seq`, `Ui::released`, `Ui::track_moves`, `Ui::settle`).
